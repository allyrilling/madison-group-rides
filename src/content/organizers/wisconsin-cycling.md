UW–Madison's student cycling club and racing team, competing in the Midwest Collegiate Cycling Conference and USA Cycling Collegiate Nationals as a Division I program. Also welcomes non-racing leisure riders. Madison is set to host USA Cycling Collegiate Road Nationals in May 2026, with this team central to it.
