A Dane County road racing and riding club (including its USAC-affiliated "MadCity Racing" team) running April–September, with a $25/year membership and a volunteer expectation at club events. Sponsors the Badfish Creek Time Trial and the L'Alpe Bl'Huez Hill Climb in Blue Mounds. Members get a discount at Machinery Row Bicycles.
