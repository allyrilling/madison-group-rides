A long-running, leaderless, volunteer-organized weekly road ride through rural Dane County, April–October, departing around 5:30 PM from one of 13 rotating start points around Madison. Routes run 20–35 hilly miles at a pace of roughly 10+ mph on hills, unsupported. Free, no membership required.
