# Madison, WI Group Bike Rides — Research Notes

Compiled September 2026 via web search/fetch. Confidence and currency vary by
entry — see notes on each. Distances/times/pace are as advertised by the
organizer; verify before publishing anything as fact-checked/official.

---

## Recurring Rides

### WHHDS (Wingra, Heron, Hops and Derailleur Society) — multiple weekly rides
- **Organizer:** Informal club, coordinates via Groups.IO / Facebook / Discord (Zwift)
- **Type:** Recurring, seasonal (outdoor Apr–Oct, indoor Zwift Nov–Mar)
- **Schedule (outdoor season):**
  - Monday 6:00 AM — "EEOTA" start — ~20 mi, 16–20 mph, flat/easy, beginner-friendly
  - Tuesday 5:30 AM — "The Buffalo" start — ~20 mi, 16–20 mph, rolling hills, vigorous
  - Thursday 5:30 AM — "WEOTA" start — ~20 mi, 16–20 mph, rolling (Paoli loop variant)
  - Friday 5:30 AM — "The Buffalo" start — ~15 mi, 16–20 mph, mostly bike paths, low-stress/social
  - Saturday — time varies, posted weekly — 20–40 mi, 17–20 mph
  - "Dirty Wednesday" mountain bike ride, May–mid-August, ride-your-own-ability
  - Women on Wheels (WoW): Tue & Thu 6:00 PM, Lake Farm County Park, 17+ mph, Apr–Oct
- **Indoor season:** Zwift group rides Mon/Wed/Fri 6:00–7:00 AM Central, Nov–Mar
- **Terrain:** Road, mixed with some path
- **Cost:** Free
- **Notes:** "The Buffalo," "EEOTA," "WEOTA" are informal local landmark names used by the group — exact street addresses were not confirmed on the site; would need direct confirmation before publishing precise start points.
- **Source:** https://whhds.org/ (fetched directly, appears current/actively maintained)

### Brazen Dropouts — "Row Rides"
- **Organizer:** Brazen Dropouts (informal team/club)
- **Type:** Recurring, seasonal (May–Sept 2026)
- **Schedule:** Every other Saturday, 9:00 AM. 2026 dates listed: 5/9, 5/23, 6/6, 6/20, 7/18, 8/1, 8/15, 8/29, 9/12, 9/26 (no ride 7/4).
- **Start location:** Machinery Row Bicycles, 601 Williamson St, Madison, WI 53703
- **Distance/pace:** 36 miles; group targets ~20 mph average on the non-path portion; three optional sprint segments; casual roll through the UW Arboretum at the end. Occasional Sunday long rides, 40–70 mi at ~18–19 mph, route varies with weather/wind.
- **Terrain:** Road, mixed urban/rural
- **Cost:** Free, open to everyone
- **Source:** http://www.brazendropouts.org/row-rides (fetched directly, looks current — has specific 2026 dates)

### Machinery Row Bicycles — Century Training Club
- **Organizer:** Machinery Row Bicycles (shop)
- **Type:** Recurring, seasonal
- **Schedule:** Sundays, 8:00 AM, through September; starts ~20 mi in mid-May, builds to ~70 mi by late August
- **Start location:** Machinery Row Bicycles, 601 Williamson St, Madison, WI 53703
- **Pace/level:** Intermediate riders, no fixed mph given
- **Terrain:** Road
- **Cost:** Free (join shop's group-ride email list via lauren@machineryrowbicycles.com)
- **Source:** https://www.machineryrowbicycles.com/articles/group-rides-pg249.htm

### Machinery Row Bicycles — Lunch Bunch E-Bike Rides
- **Organizer:** Machinery Row Bicycles (shop)
- **Type:** Recurring
- **Schedule:** Fridays, 12:00–2:00 PM
- **Start location:** Machinery Row Bicycles, 601 Williamson St, Madison, WI 53703
- **Pace:** Casual, includes a lunch stop; e-bike rentals available for the ride
- **Terrain:** Road/path, casual
- **Cost:** Free to join; rental fee if you need an e-bike
- **Source:** https://www.machineryrowbicycles.com/articles/group-rides-pg249.htm

### Bombay Bicycle Club — Road ride program (Thu/Sat/Sun/Mon)
- **Organizer:** Bombay Bicycle Club (Madison's largest recreational cycling club, ClubExpress-run)
- **Type:** Recurring, seasonal (outdoor Apr–Nov; indoor Zwift Nov–Mar)
- **Schedule (general pattern, exact routes/times posted weekly on RideWithGPS):**
  - Thursday evening — any pace, group meal afterward
  - Saturday morning — social/chill pace with coffee stop
  - Sunday morning — hillier routes
  - Monday morning — chill pace with picnic lunch after
- **Cost:** Paid club membership (amount not confirmed — check bombaybicycle.org)
- **Notes:** Official site (bombaybicycle.org) blocked automated fetch (403), so exact current start locations/times weren't directly retrievable — schedule described here is from search-result summaries and should be verified against their live RideWithGPS schedule before publishing precise times/locations. This is likely the single largest source of recurring road rides in the area.
- **Source:** https://bombaybicycle.org/ (blocked); summarized via search snippets of bombaybicycle.org pages

### Madison Women's Cycling Club (MWCC)
- **Organizer:** Madison Women's Cycling Club (open to women, trans, and non-binary cyclists)
- **Type:** Recurring, seasonal
- **Schedule:** Tuesdays, 6:00 PM; two ride options each week (routes posted on Instagram closer to the date)
- **Start location:** Commonly Olbrich Park (Biergarten parking lot, lake side of Atwood Ave)
- **Distance/pace:** Social route ~12 mi lake loop, includes a sweep rider so no one is dropped
- **Terrain:** Road/path, casual-to-social
- **Cost:** Free
- **Source:** Facebook group (facebook.com/groups/422391611714723), Instagram @madisonwomenscyclingclub, corroborated across multiple search snippets

### Cap City Cyclists — "Mad Town Mondays"
- **Organizer:** Cap City Cyclists (new club, founded 2024)
- **Type:** Recurring, seasonal
- **Schedule:** Mondays, 6:00 PM
- **Start location:** Orton Park, 1100 Spaight St, Madison, WI
- **Distance/pace:** 10–12 mph "party pace," described as inclusive/welcoming to all levels. Usually an 18-mi loop to McFarland/Lake Waubesa boardwalk, or a shorter 12-mi Lake Monona loop in bad weather/low light. Roughly monthly there's a longer 30–40 mi ride in summer.
- **Terrain:** Road
- **Cost:** Free; a "Group Ride Waiver" form is requested
- **Notes:** Site (capcitycyclists.com) itself says the ride is still being "finalized" — a newer, possibly less established group than Bombay/WHHDS/Brazen Dropouts. Confirm current status via their Instagram (@cap.city.cyclists) before publishing as active.
- **Source:** https://www.capcitycyclists.com/mad-town-mondays

### Madison Queer Bike Ride
- **Organizer:** Community-run (via Instagram @madisonqueerbikeride), covered by Our Lives Wisconsin
- **Type:** Recurring, year-round
- **Schedule:** Second Wednesday of every month
- **Start location:** Law Park (near the boat launch/"tiny beach"), Madison, WI
- **Pace/level:** Explicitly welcomes all bodies, including new/infrequent riders
- **Terrain:** Road/path, casual
- **Cost:** Free
- **Source:** https://ourliveswisconsin.com/article/madison-queer-bike-ride/, Instagram @madisonqueerbikeride

### Radical Adventure Riders (RAR) — Madison chapter
- **Organizer:** Radical Adventure Riders, national org focused on gender inclusivity & racial equity in bike adventure spaces; Madison is one of ~11 chapters
- **Type:** Recurring (weekly meetings/rides), but chapter is relatively new
- **Schedule:** Weekly meetings, community dinners, and rides (both sober and non-sober events offered) — specific day/time/location not confirmed in research; check Instagram
- **Cost:** Likely free/donation
- **Notes:** Described as a newly formed collective as of the source article; verify current activity level before publishing.
- **Source:** https://radicaladventureriders.com/chapters/madison; https://madison.com/news/local/breaking-the-cycle-new-bike-club-seeks-to-make-madisons-cycling-community-more-inclusive/

### CORP (Capital Off Road Pathfinders) — group mountain bike rides
- **Organizer:** CORP / MadCityDirt (IMBA chapter for the Madison area)
- **Type:** Recurring
- **Schedule:** Rides depart the "CORP Garage," 301 W Water St, Cambridge, WI, at "6 bells" (6 PM), riding 1–2 hours depending on the group. Days not precisely confirmed from the pages fetched — check madcitydirt.com / corptrails.org events page directly.
- **Locations ridden:** Quarry Park, Quarry Ridge, CamRock, and other south-central WI trail systems
- **Terrain:** Mountain bike / singletrack
- **Cost:** Free to ride; membership dues support trail building/maintenance
- **Source:** https://www.singletracks.com/bike-clubs/capitol-off-road-pathfinders-corp/, http://www.madcitydirt.com/, https://corptrails.org/join (join page had no ride specifics; check /events)

## One-Time / Annual Events

### Horribly Hilly Hundreds
- **Date (2026):** Saturday, June 13, 2026, 6:45 AM start
- **Location:** Blue Mounds, WI (Blue Mound State Park area)
- **Distances:** 100K, 150K, 200K
- **Organizer:** Friends of Blue Mound State Park
- **Description:** A well-known, hilly road century-distance event through the Driftless region west of Madison.
- **Cost/registration:** Via BikeSignUp — waitlist has existed in past years, register early
- **Source:** https://www.bikesignup.com/Race/WI/BlueMounds/HorribleHillyHundreds, https://www.friendsofbluemound.org/horribly-hilly-hundreds-bike-ride/, https://www.ahotu.com/event/horribly-hilly-hundreds

### Trek 100
- **Date (2026):** Saturday, June 20, 2026; rollouts at 7:30 AM (100 mi), 8:30 AM (66 mi), 9:30 AM (33 mi / 20 mi)
- **Location:** Trek global headquarters, Waterloo, WI (~25 mi east of Madison)
- **Distances:** 100 mi, 66 mi, 33 mi, 20 mi loop-style course
- **Organizer:** Trek Bicycle Corporation
- **Description:** Long-running charity ride benefiting the MACC Fund (pediatric cancer/blood disorder research) and the Wisconsin Bike Fed; 2026 edition ties into Trek's 50th anniversary with a Friday night party.
- **Cost/registration:** Via trekbikes.com/trek100
- **Source:** https://www.trekbikes.com/us/en_US/trek100/, https://wisconsinbikefed.org/events/trek-100/, https://maccfund.org/trek-100/

### Wright Stuff Century
- **Date (2026):** Sunday, September 6, 2026, 7:00 AM start
- **Location:** Dane County, WI (exact start venue not confirmed — likely near Spring Green/Taliesin area given route description); organized by Bombay Bicycle Club
- **Distances:** Century (100 mi), with a shorter south loop option to Blanchardville
- **Organizer:** Bombay Bicycle Club (their signature annual event, 49th year in 2026)
- **Description:** Route passes Frank Lloyd Wright's Taliesin and Unity Chapel through the Driftless region's hills; proceeds benefit Bikes for Kids Wisconsin.
- **Cost/registration:** Via bombaybicycle.org
- **Source:** https://wisconsinbikefed.org/events/wright-stuff-century-2/, https://isthmus.com/events/wright-stuff-century-ride-annual/ (blocked, corroborated via WI Bike Fed)

### Loop the Lake
- **Date (2026):** Saturday, June 20, 2026 (note: same weekend as Trek 100 — conflict to flag)
- **Time:** Optional group start 9:00 AM, event runs 9 AM–2 PM
- **Location:** Starts/ends at The East Side Club, 3735 Monona Drive, Madison, WI
- **Distance:** 12-mile scenic ride around/near Lake Monona
- **Organizer:** Clean Lakes Alliance (nonprofit)
- **Description:** Family-friendly fundraiser ride supporting lake health/water quality work in the Madison lakes district; includes a post-ride cookout picnic.
- **Cost/registration:** Via bikesignup.com or cleanlakesalliance.org
- **Source:** https://www.cleanlakesalliance.org/loop-the-lake/, https://www.bikesignup.com/Race/WI/Madison/LooptheLakeBikeRide2025, https://ibmadison.com/loop-the-lake-support-healthier-lakes/

## Clubs & Organizations (no single fixed ride, but worth listing as contacts/hosts)

### Bombay Bicycle Club
- Madison's largest recreational cycling club; runs the Thu/Sat/Sun/Mon road ride program above plus the Wright Stuff Century. Paid membership via ClubExpress.
- https://bombaybicycle.org/

### Wisconsin Cycling Club (UW–Madison student club)
- Competitive/social collegiate club racing MWCCC Division I in cyclocross, mountain, and road disciplines; also runs non-competitive social rides open to all skill levels. $50/year dues via DoSportsEasy.
- https://wisconsincycling.org/, https://win.wisc.edu/organization/wisconsincyclingclub

### Wisconsin Hoofers (Hoofer Riding/Outing Club, UW–Madison)
- Large (2,000+ member) UW student org; includes mountain biking programming (see hooferouting.org/mountain-biking) alongside its other outdoor-rec programs. Road cycling program details were less clear from search results — the "Wisconsin Cycling Club" appears to be the more road/race-focused UW group.
- https://www.hooferouting.org/mountain-biking, https://union.wisc.edu/events-and-activities/outdoor-uw/wisconsin-hoofers

### CORP (Capital Off Road Pathfinders) / MadCityDirt
- The area's IMBA-affiliated mountain bike advocacy org — trail building/maintenance plus group rides (see Recurring Rides above) and skills clinics.
- https://corptrails.org/, http://www.madcitydirt.com/

### CamRock MTB, Inc.
- 501(c)(3) nonprofit focused on youth MTB (student-athlete) programming in Jefferson/Dane counties, based around CamRock County Park; also runs the community Thursday Night Rides.
- https://camrock.org/

### Wisconsin Bike Fed
- Statewide cycling advocacy nonprofit; maintains the best general event calendar for the state (including Madison-area rides/races) and organizes several signature events (Wisconsin Bike Week, Madison Pride Ride support, etc.).
- https://wisconsinbikefed.org/events/

### Radical Adventure Riders — Madison chapter
- Gender-inclusive/racial-equity-focused adventure cycling collective; newer chapter, weekly meetings/dinners/rides.
- https://radicaladventureriders.com/chapters/madison

### Cap City Cyclists
- New (founded 2024) Madison cycling/advocacy group; currently mainly known for Mad Town Mondays but describes itself as still building out programming.
- https://www.capcitycyclists.com/

### Wisconsin Gravel Syndicate
- Facebook-based gravel riding community; runs occasional programming (e.g., bikepacking intro nights, a gear lending library) but no confirmed *regular* weekly ride schedule was found. Treat as a loose community/contact rather than a scheduled ride.
- https://www.facebook.com/WisconsinGravelSyndicate/

### Madison Freewheel Bicycle Co. ("Freewheel")
- 501(c)(3) community bike shop/education nonprofit (transportation-justice focused); teaches bike repair via Open Shop hours. No group rides confirmed — more of a resource/education org than a ride organizer, but worth linking as a community resource.
- https://freewheelbikes.org/, 31 S Mills St, Madison, WI 53715

### Bikes for Kids Wisconsin
- Nonprofit bike shop/co-op (used bikes, youth access); beneficiary of the Wright Stuff Century proceeds. Worth listing as a related community org even though it isn't itself a ride organizer.
- https://bikesforkidswi.org/
